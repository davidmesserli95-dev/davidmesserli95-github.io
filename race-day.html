Before you go

  • Confirm details: details for 2026–27 come from each race's site as of November 2026. Re-check the official page the week of the race, especially start times and packet pickup hours.
  • Tortoise & Hare: FCRC membership is required. Registration opens 3 weeks before each race and closes at noon the Friday before. There's no race-day registration.
  • Night before: pin your bib on, lay out clothes for the start temperature, and charge your watch.
